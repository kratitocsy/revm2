1. Who we are

Wynko ("Wynko", "we", "us", "our") is a collaborative learning platform for students, available at https://wynko.in and through our apps (together, the "Platform"). Wynko helps students focus, study solo, join study chat and voice rooms, set goals, and learn together.

Wynko is currently operated as an unregistered venture by its founding team. The person responsible for your personal data is:

  • Operator: Mahipat Singh Sengar, operating as Wynko
  • Address: 154 Sevagram Colony, Khajuraho, Madhya Pradesh, India
  • Contact email: founder@wynko.in
  • Grievance Officer (India — required under the DPDP Act, 2023 and the IT Rules, 2021): Mahipat Singh Sengar, founder@wynko.in

If Wynko is later incorporated as a company, this Policy will be updated to name that company as the data fiduciary and your data will transfer to it.

2. Scope

This Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It applies to everyone who uses the Platform, including people who only sign up to the waitlist. It does not apply to third-party websites we link to.

3.3 Voice rooms — how your audio is handled

Voice study rooms run on Agora, a real-time audio provider. When you join a voice room:

  • Your audio is transmitted live through Agora's servers to the other people in that room.
  • We do not record or store your voice. Audio is streamed in real time, is not saved on Wynko's servers, and Agora's cloud recording is not enabled.
  • Agora processes technical call data (connection quality, timing, IP address, channel ID) to deliver and debug the call. It acts as our processor and may not use your audio for its own purposes.
  • Your microphone is accessed only while you are in a voice room and have granted browser or device permission. You can mute or leave at any time, and you can revoke microphone permission in your browser or device settings.
  • If we ever introduce recording, we will ask for your explicit consent first and show a clear recording indicator to everyone in the room.

3.4 Sensitive data we don't want

Please do not put sensitive personal data — health details, Aadhaar or other government ID numbers, bank account numbers, caste or religion — into your profile, notes, or chat messages. We don't need it and we ask you not to share it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with anyone for their own advertising. We do not run behavioural advertising on the Platform.

5. Users under 18 — important

The minimum age to use Wynko is 13.

Under India's DPDP Act, everyone under 18 is treated as a "child". This means:

  • If you are under 18, you may only use Wynko with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, and we will ask you to provide a parent's or guardian's verifiable consent before your account is fully activated.
  • We do not carry out behavioural tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising directed at users under 18.
  • We keep data collected from under-18 users to the minimum needed to run the study features, and we never record voice rooms.
  • Voice rooms let minors speak with other users in real time. Parents and guardians should be aware of this; we act quickly on safety reports sent to founder@wynko.in.
  • Parents and guardians can review, correct, export, or delete their child's data, or withdraw consent, by emailing founder@wynko.in. We will act within 30 days.

If we learn that we hold data about a child without the required parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies and local storage — login sessions, security, and load balancing (Supabase auth, Cloudflare). These can't be turned off.
  • Preference cookies — remembering your settings, theme, and language.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics, to see which features are used.

We don't run advertising or retargeting pixels. Where the law requires it, we ask for consent before setting analytics cookies, and you can change your choice at any time by emailing founder@wynko.in or through your browser settings.

10. How we protect your data

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS everywhere, via Cloudflare and Vercel) and at rest with our storage providers. We use Supabase row-level security and role-based access, restrict access to the minimum number of people, keep dependencies patched, and log access. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India (and CERT-In, and your EU/UK authority where applicable) within the legally required timeframes.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access the data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Delete your data;
  • Port your data to another service in a machine-readable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't undo processing already done lawfully);
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (DPDP Act);
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or to your local authority if you are in the EU/EEA or UK.

Email founder@wynko.in. We respond within 30 days and never penalise you for exercising a right. We may ask you to verify your identity first. You can delete your account at any time by emailing founder@wynko.in from your registered email address.
