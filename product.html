Built for better study

Everything you need to study with focus.

From planning your syllabus to blocking distractions, revising at the right time, studying with others, and competing in battles — Wynko brings your study workflow together.

Automatic study plans

Give Wynko your exam date and syllabus. It builds a structured study plan and adapts as you progress.

Distraction-free study

Choose the apps and websites you want blocked during a focus session and study without digital distractions.

Automated revision

Wynko schedules revision at the right time using your study progress and retention patterns, so important topics don't get forgotten.

Study together

Join subject-based study rooms, study alongside other students, and stay accountable without studying alone.

Compete. Challenge. Improve.

Turn studying into a challenge. Join study battles, compete with others, track your performance, and earn achievements.

Know your progress

Track your focus time, study consistency, revision progress, and performance in one place.
