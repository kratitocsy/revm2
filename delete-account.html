Delete your Wynko account and data

Wynko — Learn Together. Win Together. · wynko.in

This page explains how to ask Wynko to delete your account and the personal data in it. It applies to the Wynko Android app, the Wynko website and the Wynko desktop app. Deleting your account is free.

How to delete your account

Option 1: in the Wynko Android app

  1. Open the Wynko app and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm when asked.

Option 2: by email

  1. Write to founder@wynko.in from the email address registered on your account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Wynko account.
  3. If you can no longer sign in, tell us the email address or username on the account. We may ask you to confirm it is yours before we delete anything.

We delete the account within 30 days of your request, and usually sooner.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in record (name, email address, profile picture and user ID).
  • Your profile and study records: goals, study history, streaks, saved blocks and schedules.
  • Files and content you uploaded.

Our backups are purged on a rolling basis within 35 days of deletion.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Payment and tax records: 8 years, as Indian tax law requires (once paid plans are available).
  • Security and abuse logs: up to 12 months, to keep Wynko safe.
  • Messages you posted in a study room may stay visible to other members of that room for up to 12 months, as set out in our Privacy Policy.

If we cannot delete something, we will tell you what and why.

Delete only some of your data

If you want to keep your account but remove something, such as your profile picture, uploaded files or chat history, email founder@wynko.in and tell us what to remove.

More information

Read our full Privacy Policy, or contact us at founder@wynko.in.
